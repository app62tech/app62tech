This site is deliberately small: four pages, one form, no accounts, no database.

## What we collect

**Contact form.** If you submit the contact form on our home page, we collect your name, email address, the message you write, and optionally which service you're asking about. That submission is sent as an email to our inbox via [Resend](https://resend.com), a transactional email provider. We may also send you a one-off confirmation email that your message arrived, and post a copy of your submission to our team's private chat (Telegram or Slack) so we see it quickly. We do not store submissions in a database — beyond our inbox and that team notification, no copy is kept.

**Analytics.** We use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to see how many people visit the site and which pages they read. It does not use cookies or any persistent identifier, and does not track you across other sites. No consent banner is shown because none is required for this kind of measurement.

**Spam protection.** The contact form uses [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/) to filter automated spam submissions. Turnstile may process technical signals about your browser and connection as part of that check; see [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for how it handles that.

## What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. We don't run advertising trackers. We don't use Google Analytics.
